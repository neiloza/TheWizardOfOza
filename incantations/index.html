Spell One

Website Building

You need a website. I build websites. The negotiation is basically over. Portfolio, business site, or full e-commerce platform—I conjure a digital home that looks sharp, loads before your visitor finishes blinking, and quietly convinces browsers to become customers. Building Video Games

Spell Three

Data Insights

Your spreadsheet knows things. It has seen things. It will talk—to me. With Python, SQL, and an interrogation lamp, I turn raw numbers into dashboards, statistical analyses, predictive models, and visualizations so clear your least numerate stakeholder will nod with genuine understanding. Customer behavior, trend forecasts, operational bottlenecks, opportunities hiding in column Q—I find the story your data has been mumbling and make it say it out loud, with charts.

Python SQL Dashboards Forecasting Visualization
Unlock Your Data's Potential
Spell Four

Teaching & Consulting

Want the hat? The hat can be taught. I offer one-on-one teaching and mentorship in coding, mathematics, game development, web design, and data science—tailored to your pace, whether you are typing your first line of code or leveling up something specific. Lessons are project-based and hands-on, which means you build real things and gain the dangerous confidence of a person who has debugged their own work at midnight and won.

I also consult for businesses that would rather not learn technology lessons the expensive way. Technical strategy, architecture reviews, UX audits, performance optimization—I point at the thing that is about to cost you money and suggest, gently but firmly, that we fix it first.
