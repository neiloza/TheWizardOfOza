About Serenity Yoga

Our story, philosophy, and dedicated instructors

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Serenity Yoga Studio emerged from a simple vision: to create a sanctuary where people could reconnect with themselves through the transformative practice of yoga.

What began as a single room with a handful of students has blossomed into Daytona Beach's beloved yoga community, serving hundreds of practitioners each week.

We honor traditional yoga wisdom while embracing modern understanding of movement and wellness, creating an inclusive space where everyone—regardless of age, body type, or experience—feels welcome.

Our Philosophy

Mindful Practice

We emphasize present-moment awareness, bringing attention to breath, body, and inner experience

Compassionate Guidance

Our teachers offer modifications and variations, honoring each student's unique journey

Continuous Growth

Yoga is a lifelong practice; we support exploration, curiosity, and gradual development

Community Connection

We foster authentic relationships, creating bonds that extend beyond the mat

Meet Our Instructors

Maya Chen

Founder & Lead Instructor

Maya discovered yoga during a challenging period in her corporate career and found it so transformative that she left the business world to teach. With over 1,000 hours of training in Vinyasa, Yin, and Restorative yoga, she brings both technical expertise and heartfelt passion to every class.

Certifications: E-RYT 500, Yoga Alliance

James Rodriguez

Power Yoga & Strength

A former athlete, James integrates his background in sports medicine with dynamic yoga practice. His classes challenge students to build strength and flexibility while maintaining mindful awareness. Known for his encouraging teaching style and creative sequencing.

Certifications: RYT 500, NASM Personal Trainer

Sarah Williams

Yin Yoga & Meditation

Sarah's gentle, introspective approach creates space for deep release and inner exploration. Trained in traditional yoga philosophy and modern mindfulness practices, she guides students toward greater self-awareness and emotional balance.

Certifications: RYT 200, Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction Teacher
