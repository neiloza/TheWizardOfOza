Vinyasa Flow

All Levels

Dynamic, flowing sequences that synchronize breath with movement. Build strength, flexibility, and mindfulness as you move through creative poses.

Beginner Friendly

Slow-paced, meditative practice with poses held for 3-5 minutes. Target deep connective tissues and promote relaxation and flexibility.

Duration: 75 minutes

Intermediate

Vigorous, fitness-based approach that builds strength, stamina, and mental focus. Expect challenging sequences and dynamic movement.

Restorative Yoga

Gentle practice using props to support the body in restful poses. Perfect for stress relief, healing, and deep relaxation.
