About Aaron

A Proven Conservative Leader

Aaron Baker isn't a career politician — he's a Florida native who understands the challenges facing working families because he lives them every day. Born in Lakeland and now calling Sorrento home, Aaron is the only candidate in this race who actually lives in the 6th District.

As a business owner and community leader, Aaron has spent his career creating jobs, supporting local infrastructure, and fighting for conservative values. He's ready to take that same work ethic to Washington to fight for you.
