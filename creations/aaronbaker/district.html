About the 6th District

Florida's 6th Congressional District encompasses beautiful communities across Central Florida, including parts of Volusia, Lake, and Flagler counties. From the Atlantic coast to the inland lakes region, this district is home to hardworking families, retirees, veterans, and small business owners who deserve strong conservative representation in Washington.

Major Communities

Deltona

One of Florida's largest cities, home to diverse families and growing businesses

Daytona Beach

World-famous beach destination and motorsports capital

Port Orange

Thriving coastal community with excellent quality of life

Ormond Beach

Historic beach town known for its charm and strong community

Sorrento

Small-town Florida living in the heart of the lakes region

Palm Coast

Rapidly growing city with beautiful parks and trails
