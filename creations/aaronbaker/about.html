About Aaron Baker

A Florida Native Fighting for Conservative Values

Born and Raised in Central Florida

Aaron Baker was born in Lakeland, Florida, and has spent his entire life serving the communities of Central Florida. Unlike career politicians who parachute into districts for political gain, Aaron actually lives in Florida's 6th Congressional District — in Sorrento, where he's raising his family and building his future.

Growing up in a working-class family, Aaron learned the values of hard work, personal responsibility, and service to community. He watched his parents work multiple jobs to provide for their family, and those lessons shaped his understanding of the challenges facing everyday Floridians.

A Conservative Business Leader

As a successful business owner, Aaron knows firsthand the burden that excessive regulation and taxation place on job creators. He's built his career on conservative principles: fiscal responsibility, limited government, and free-market solutions to economic challenges.

Aaron has created dozens of jobs in Central Florida, invested in local infrastructure projects, and worked tirelessly to improve his community. He understands that government doesn't create prosperity — hardworking Americans and free enterprise do.

Fighting for Our District

Aaron is running for Congress because he's tired of politicians in Washington who put party politics ahead of the people. He believes in putting America First, securing our border, cutting wasteful spending, and protecting the constitutional rights that make our country great.

Most importantly, Aaron will never forget where he came from. He's not running to become a career politician — he's running to be a voice for the hardworking families, small business owners, and patriots of Florida's 6th District.

"I'm not a politician — I'm a Floridian who's tired of watching Washington fail our families. It's time to send a conservative fighter to Congress who will actually represent our values."

— Aaron Baker
