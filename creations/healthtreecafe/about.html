About Health Tree Cafe

Rooted in wellness, growing with our community

Our Story

Health Tree Cafe was born from a simple belief: that healthy food should be delicious, accessible, and made with ingredients you can trust. Located in the heart of Daytona Beach at 603 N Beach St, we've become a gathering place for those who value wellness without compromising on taste.

Every dish we serve reflects our commitment to organic, locally-sourced ingredients prepared fresh daily. From our vibrant smoothie bowls to our nourishing salads and power-packed wraps, each item on our menu is crafted with care and attention to nutritional value.

We believe that eating well should be a joy, not a chore. That's why our team works tirelessly to create meals that fuel your body and delight your taste buds. Whether you're a longtime health enthusiast or just beginning your wellness journey, you'll find something to love at Health Tree Cafe.
