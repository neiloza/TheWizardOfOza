About HandyConnect

Making home services simple, transparent, and trustworthy

Our Mission

HandyConnect was founded with a simple belief: finding reliable home service professionals shouldn't be stressful. We've all experienced the frustration of endless phone calls, unreturned messages, and uncertainty about who to trust with our homes.

Our platform connects homeowners with vetted, professional contractors and handymen who are ready to help. We handle the vetting, scheduling, and payment processing so you can focus on what matters—getting your project done right.

Our Values

Trust

Every professional on our platform is thoroughly vetted, background-checked, and verified.

Transparency

Clear pricing, honest reviews, and open communication at every step of your project.

Quality

We only work with skilled professionals who take pride in their craftsmanship.

Community

Building lasting relationships between homeowners and trusted local professionals.
