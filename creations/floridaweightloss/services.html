Medical Weight Loss with Semaglutide & Tirzepatide

FDA-approved prescription medications for effective, medically-supervised weight loss.

For those who want or need additional support beyond natural methods, we offer the latest FDA-approved weight loss medications. Semaglutide (branded as Ozempic and Wegovy) and Tirzepatide (branded as Mounjaro and Zepbound) are GLP-1 receptor agonists that have shown remarkable results in clinical trials and real-world use.

How These Medications Work:

These medications mimic natural hormones in your body that regulate appetite, blood sugar, and digestion. They work by reducing hunger, increasing feelings of fullness, slowing stomach emptying, and improving insulin sensitivity. The result is significant weight loss with relatively minimal side effects for most people.

What's Included:

  • Medical Evaluation: Comprehensive health assessment to determine if you're a good candidate
  • Prescription Management: Expert dosing and medication management by our healthcare team
  • Regular Monitoring: Ongoing health monitoring to track progress and adjust as needed
  • Injection Training: Detailed instruction on self-administration (it's easier than you think!)
  • Nutritional Guidance: Dietary recommendations to maximize results
  • Side Effect Management: Support and strategies to minimize any discomfort
  • Supplies Included: All necessary injection supplies provided

Semaglutide vs. Tirzepatide:

Semaglutide: The original GLP-1 medication that has helped millions lose significant weight. Well-studied with a proven track record. Weekly injection.

Tirzepatide: The newest option that works on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors. Studies show even greater weight loss than Semaglutide for many patients. Weekly injection.

During your consultation, we'll discuss which medication is best suited to your needs, health history, and goals.

Expected Results:

Clinical studies show average weight loss of 15-20% of body weight over 6-12 months. Many of our clients see 30-50+ pounds of weight loss. Results vary by individual.

Body Contouring with Red Light Therapy

Non-invasive fat reduction and skin tightening to complement your weight loss.

As you lose weight, you may find areas where stubborn fat persists or where skin needs tightening. Our advanced Red Light Therapy (also called Near-Infrared Light Therapy or Lipo Light) provides a safe, painless, non-surgical solution to target these problem areas.

How It Works:

Red Light Therapy uses specific wavelengths of light to penetrate the skin and stimulate fat cells. This causes the fat cells to release their contents, which are then naturally processed and eliminated by your body. The treatment also stimulates collagen production, helping to tighten and tone the skin.

Treatment Benefits:

  • Non-Invasive: No surgery, no needles, no incisions
  • Painless: Most clients find the treatment relaxing
  • No Downtime: Return to normal activities immediately
  • Targeted Fat Loss: Focus on specific problem areas like abdomen, thighs, arms
  • Skin Tightening: Improves skin texture and reduces sagging
  • Cellulite Reduction: Helps smooth the appearance of cellulite
  • Quick Sessions: Treatments typically take 20-30 minutes

Best Results When Combined With:

Body contouring works best as a complement to weight loss, not a replacement. We recommend combining it with either our Natural or Medical Weight Loss programs for optimal results. The fat reduction from light therapy plus dietary changes creates comprehensive body transformation.

Treatment Protocol:

Most clients see best results with 2-3 sessions per week for 4-8 weeks. We'll customize a treatment plan based on your specific goals and target areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost?

Pricing varies based on the program and your specific needs. We offer options at different price points to accommodate various budgets. The best way to get accurate pricing is to schedule a free consultation where we can discuss your goals and recommend the most appropriate program.

How fast will I lose weight?

Most clients lose 2-5 pounds per week depending on the program. Natural weight loss tends to be 2-3 pounds per week, while medical weight loss with Semaglutide or Tirzepatide often results in 3-5 pounds per week, especially in the first few months.

Do I have to exercise?

No! While exercise is healthy and we encourage it, our programs are designed to work without requiring intense workouts. Many clients lose significant weight before they ever step foot in a gym.

Will I have to give up the foods I love?

No deprivation required. Our approach teaches you how to enjoy a wide variety of foods while still losing weight. We focus on food quality and metabolic optimization, not restriction.

What if I've tried everything and nothing works?

You're not alone. Most of our clients have tried multiple diets before finding success with us. Our approach is different because we address the metabolic reasons why you've struggled, not just cut calories.

Are the medications safe?

Semaglutide and Tirzepatide are FDA-approved and have been used safely by millions of people. All medications have potential side effects, which we'll discuss thoroughly during your consultation. Our medical team monitors you closely throughout treatment.
