The Webweaver's Vision

2.5 million visits walked into a web. Here is where they all went.

Project Overview

A spider knows every thread of its web. This project applies the same energy to website traffic: twelve months of user journeys and conversion funnels, mapped until every wander, hesitation, and rage-quit had an explanation.

Tracking behavior across every touchpoint surfaced the unglamorous truths that actually move revenue: which pages perform, which pages hemorrhage visitors, and precisely where the conversion funnel springs its leaks.

The result is a punch list, not a poem: concrete UX fixes, content moves, and marketing adjustments, each backed by numbers instead of vibes.

Project Details

Tools Used:

Google Analytics, SQL, Looker

Data Source:

Multi-site Analytics

Time Period:

12 months

Sessions Analyzed:

2.5M+ visits

Key Insights

🛤️

User Journeys

Mapped 15 distinct user paths. Only 3 actually lead to conversion; the other 12 are scenic routes.

📉

Bounce Reduction

Found pages bouncing 65% of visitors like a strict doorman. Fixes cut it to 32%.

⚡

Load Performance

Pages loading under 2 seconds converted 45% better. Patience is not a web strategy.

📱

Mobile Traffic

68% of traffic is mobile, converting at just 42% of desktop—a thumb-sized gap worth real money.
